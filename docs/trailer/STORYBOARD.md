# Lewd Vault trailer: "One key"

## PAGE
- Problem: you keep a spicy collection on a PC other people walk past (a shared room, a work laptop).
- Audience: people who collect NSFW art and want it organised and private (18+).
- Goal: "it's a beautiful vault, and one key makes it vanish."
- Emotion: a grin, then relief.
- The one true feature: the emergency key (Ctrl+Shift+Space) turning the app into a lock-screen screensaver, and back.

## Ideas
1. **One key (chosen).** The vault in use, real UI; "Someone at the door?"; the three real keycaps get pressed; hard cut to the calm blue lock screen (a held joke); pressed again, back to the vault; the camera ends on the app's own heart lockup. Cover the logo: the film is still about this app's key, which a competitor does not have.
2. Real-output montage: tags typed into the search, the grid filtering, the slideshow climbing levels. Product-true but category-generic (any gallery app).
3. Typographic essay on privacy ("No thumbnails. No caches. No traces."): slogan triplet, slide grammar. Rejected.
Picked 1: it is the product's own success moment, it is funny, and the cut itself is the proof.

## World
- Act 1 (vault): ground is the real app, magnified by a camera that keeps creeping; rose light from the UI itself; depth from the push.
- Act 2 (hidden): ground is the app's own screensaver (its CSS: drifting aurora blobs, big clock), cold blue; the one living thing is the aurora drift.
- Act 3 (back + close): the real app again; the camera pushes into the sidebar lockup.
- Sound: none (no ElevenLabs key); the film is cut to picture.

## Hero moment
The space bar bottoming out over the slideshow and, one frame later, the whole picture is a dull blue clock.
